SWISS STYLE FAQS (ACCORDION)

WHY ARE THERE NO GRADIENTS OR DROP SHADOWS?
Swiss Style relies entirely on pure typography, proportion, scale, and grid alignment. Shadows and gradients are considered unnecessary decorative noise.
WHAT IS THE ROLE OF NEGATIVE SPACE (WHITE SPACE)?
Negative space is not empty space; it is an active structural element that creates breathing room and focal contrast.
